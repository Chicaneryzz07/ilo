chapter one · about her

Ilo, or Iloni when I want to sound extra serious, is a whole contradiction wrapped in a black dress and a coffee order she's very particular about. She looks unbothered, a little dangerous even, the kind of girl people notice without knowing why. And then you actually talk to her and realize she's soft in all the places she'd never admit to.

She doesn't say "I love you" easily. She shows it instead: in the way she remembers small things, in the way she goes quiet when she actually cares, in the way her walls take forever to come down and mean everything once they do.

She loves her coffee, her lip gloss collection, her clothes, and the quiet little rituals that are hers alone.

She lives far from me, farther than I'd like, and we haven't even met in person yet. But somehow, from this distance, she's still managed to become one of my favorite people.

Baddie exterior, soft heart, terrible at saying it out loud, and somehow that's exactly who I fell for.
